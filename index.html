American Airlines Loyalty + Citi Cobrand

Havas CXLoyalty and cobrand

The AAdvantage digital experience needed modernization. The site had not been redesigned in more than a decade, and dense content, jargon, exceptions and qualifiers made a complex loyalty program difficult for customers to navigate.

The redesign refocused the experience around customer needs and made program information easier to understand. We built a data-informed design system around three principles: clear, modern and intuitive. The work also included a refreshed photography library and illustrations that help customers imagine their next trip.

The updated site gives members and prospective members a more streamlined experience, and the design system has become a standard for American Airlines’ broader digital ecosystem. I also led the rebuild of the American Airlines co-brand credit card site with Citi, covering product detail pages for prospects and existing cardmembers plus the family-of-cards page.

Havas CXAI film discovery

The festival needed to stand out in New York City’s crowded cultural landscape while drawing attention to its archive of independent films and encouraging ticket sales.

Pizza Hut Pac-Man Arcade

GSD&MWeb AR

  • FWA Site of the Day, March 27, 2021

How do you get people to play with their food before they eat it? Turn the pizza box into an arcade.

For Pizza Hut’s Pac-Man Arcade, we built a web-based augmented reality experience that brings the classic game to life on the box itself. Point a phone at the lid and the cardboard becomes a playable Pac-Man maze, complete with the ghosts, right there on the table next to the pizza. It runs in the mobile browser, so there is nothing to download between you and the first dot.

I produced the project, keeping design, development and brand partners aligned from concept through launch, and balancing a playful, nostalgic idea with the real-world demands of a web AR build that had to work on whatever phone was in someone’s hand.

The result is a mash-up of pizza night and game night, and a Site of the Day win from FWA on March 27, 2021.

Popeyes Crave-Off

GSD&MExperiential

  • 10MM people reached
  • 42.5MM impressions
  • Best in Show, ATX Addy
  • Silver, National Addy (2019)

How do you shake people out of their fast-food slump? With levitating Popeyes chicken and a mental tug-of-war, obviously.

We developed a custom table to float the $5 Big Box Combo and set two contenders, wearing brainwave-monitoring EEG headsets, against each other in a cerebral competition to see who craved Popeyes more. The one with the most craving would bring the hovering food toward themselves and enjoy victory. And it really worked!

We complemented the experience with a mobile game that connected users in real time for their own tug-of-war challenge. The project was part experiential, part interactive, part content capture, and all innovative fun.

Then we set it all loose with exciting display creative and social, reaching over 10MM people and earning 42.5MM impressions.

With Crave-Off we were able to scientifically quantify the love for Popeyes chicken.

Geico Mobile App

The Martin AgencyMobile app UX

When an app lives in the pockets of millions of customers, every tap counts.

For the GEICO Mobile app, I worked in an agile process alongside GEICO’s design and development teams to keep improving the interface and the experience. Work moved in short, focused iterations: a prioritized backlog of improvements, stories shaped and estimated with the people who would build them, regular check-ins to review progress, and a steady rhythm of releases.

As producer, I kept the teams in sync and the sprints on track. I translated business goals and customer feedback into clear, prioritized work, cleared blockers early, and balanced scope, timing and quality so design and development could move quickly without losing sight of the user.

The result was a continuous cycle of UI and UX refinements that made everyday insurance tasks simpler and more intuitive for customers on the go.
